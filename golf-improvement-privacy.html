Golf Improvement App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 (date of birth, age-based analytics defaults)

1. Who we are

The Golf Improvement App ("the app") is provided by DSIGHT CONSULTANCY GROUP LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14594769). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: connor.jones@dsightcg.com.

The app is currently in a testing (beta) phase distributed through Apple TestFlight. Features and this policy may change before public release; we will update this page when they do.

2. What the app collects

  • Account details: your email address and password are handled by our authentication provider. Our application database stores only an account identifier, the display name you choose (optional), and your app preferences (handedness, optional handicap index).
  • Date of birth: your full date of birth, entered when you create your account (or once, on your next visit, if your account pre-dates this). See section 4.
  • Golf data you enter: saved and private courses, tee and hole details you edit, rounds, scores, shot-by-shot reviews, putting details, reflections and reflection tags, and lessons you save.
  • Feedback you choose to send: if you rate an improvement priority, we store your rating, any comment you type, and a summary of the priority it relates to.
  • Optional usage information: product-use events and review-question timings, described in section 5. Never your notes, reflections or comments.
  • Subscription information: if you subscribe, Apple sends us signed transaction details (product, dates, renewal and trial status and a transaction identifier) so we can unlock Premium. We never receive your payment card or bank details.
  • Records we must keep: your acceptance of the Terms, acknowledgement of this policy, the age check described in section 4, your analytics choice, and the handling of any privacy request.
  • Security and operational logs: short-lived technical logs (for example request times and error codes) used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Why we use it and our legal bases

  • To create your account and provide the golf review and analysis you request — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep accounts and the service secure, prevent abuse and fix faults — our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable service.
  • To verify and manage a Premium subscription — contract, and our legitimate interest in preventing fraud and duplicate processing.
  • To apply age rules (section 4) — our legal obligations and legitimate interests in keeping the service age-appropriate and protecting young people.
  • To improve the app using feedback you send and product-use events (section 5) — our legitimate interests (feedback and, where permitted, usage information) or your consent where the law requires it.
  • To keep compliance records — legal obligations and legitimate interests.

The app's analysis is informational golf feedback. We do not use your data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Date of birth and age

Why we ask. We ask for your full date of birth for two reasons: (a) to make sure everyone with an account is at least 13, and (b) to apply age-appropriate privacy settings, such as switching optional analytics off by default for under-18s. When you turn 18 the adult rules apply automatically. We also use age bands only (under 18 or 18 and over), never your exact date, in combined statistics to understand who uses the app and how we can make it better for them.

What we do not do. We do not show your date of birth to anyone, share it with other users or third parties, sell it, use it for advertising or to market to you, or put it into analytics events or analytics reports (those use an age band instead).

Under 13. If the date you enter shows you are under 13, we do not create or keep the account or the date.

How long. We keep it while your account exists and delete it when you delete your account. It is included in your own data export. To correct it, use Request a correction in Settings → Privacy and your data, or email us.

5. Optional usage information (analytics)

With sharing on, the app records product-use events (for example, opening the Membership screen, starting or completing a round) and, for each review question, its name, the hole and shot, the option you chose, how long it was on screen and whether help was opened. Records are held under a random analytics ID and a scrambled round key, not your name or email. This is pseudonymised, not anonymous, data. A private link to your account is kept only so we can delete the records if you turn sharing off, delete your account, or include them in your export. We use no third-party analytics or advertising service.

  • Under 18: off by default, in every country. You will not be asked; you can switch it on yourself in Settings → Help improve the app.
  • 18 and over, United Kingdom: off until you choose. The first time you sign in we ask, with Accept and Not now given equal weight; nothing is recorded unless you accept. You can change your mind at any time in Settings.
  • 18 and over, United States: on by default (US law does not require opt-in for adults), as described in this policy, with a switch to turn it off at any time in Settings → Help improve the app. We do not sell or share this information for advertising.
  • 18 and over, elsewhere: off until you choose, as in the UK.

Turning sharing off stops new events and deletes the events and timings stored for you. Your choice is recorded so we do not ask again.

6. Sharing your analysis with a coach

Share with my coach creates a PDF and short message on your phone and opens the iPhone share sheet; you choose the recipient. It includes your display name (if set), your top mistakes, scores and patterns, never notes or reflections. We do not receive or keep a copy. Once you send it, the recipient and the service you used are responsible for their copy.

7. Reminders and location

Reminders are local notifications set on your phone. The optional “When I leave my golf club” reminder stores the club you pick (name and coordinates) on your device only and asks iOS to watch a region around it; the app does not read or upload your location. Searching for a club uses Apple Maps on your device, not our server.

8. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — account sign-in and our database, hosted in London (UK).
  • Google Cloud — hosting of the app's server, in London (UK).
  • GolfCourseAPI — when you choose an external course search, our server sends only the search text to look up course information. No personal data is sent.
  • Apple — distributes the app (TestFlight and the App Store), processes payments and subscriptions, and provides Maps search. Apple acts under its own terms and privacy policy.

These providers act on our instructions. Where a provider or its sub-processors may access data from outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. How long we keep it

We keep your account, date of birth and golf data while your account is active. When you delete your account we immediately delete your private records, date of birth, analytics records and sign-in identity, except where the law requires us to keep limited records (for example, a record that a deletion request was completed, without your golf data). Courses you contributed to the shared course catalogue may remain, without your identity. Backups and operational logs are removed on their normal short cycles.

During the testing phase, test data may be reset. We will tell testers before doing so where practical.

10. Your rights

You can export a copy of your data with Export My Data in the app's Settings, and permanently delete your account at any time with Delete account on the Settings screen. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. It does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple Account settings. You also have rights to access, correct, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to withdraw consent for optional usage information at any time. Email connor.jones@dsightcg.com to make a request.

UK: if you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

US residents (including California): you may ask to know what personal information we hold, to have it deleted or corrected, and to opt out of any sale or sharing of it. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not knowingly do so for anyone under 16. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. Use the in-app tools or email us.

11. Children and teens

The app is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13; we do not knowingly collect data from under-13s. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. For 13 to 17-year-olds we apply higher-privacy defaults: optional analytics is off unless the teenager switches it on, and we use no advertising, tracking or profiling for marketing.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to your data is restricted to your account, and server credentials are kept out of the app. No system is perfectly secure; tell us promptly if you suspect a problem.

13. Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
