Golf Improvement App Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. Who we are

The Golf Improvement App ("the app") is provided by DSIGHT CONSULTANCY GROUP LTD, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14594769). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: connor.jones@dsightcg.com.

The app is currently in a testing (beta) phase distributed through Apple TestFlight. Features and this policy may change before public release; we will update this page when they do.

2. What the app collects

  • Account details: your email address and password are handled by our authentication provider. Our application database stores only an account identifier, your display name, and your app preferences.
  • Golf data you enter: saved and private courses, tee and hole details you edit, rounds, scores, shot-by-shot reviews, reflections and reflection tags.
  • Feedback you choose to send: if you rate an improvement priority, we store your rating, any comment you type, and a summary of the priority it relates to.
  • Optional usage information: only if you turn on "Help improve the app", we record a small, fixed set of product-use events that do not include your golf data or comments. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Records we must keep: your acceptance of the Terms, acknowledgement of this policy, confirmation that you are at least 13, and the handling of any privacy request.
  • Security and operational logs: short-lived technical logs (for example request times and error codes) used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, health data, payment details or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Why we use it and our legal bases

  • To create your account and provide the golf review and analysis you request — necessary to perform our contract with you.
  • To keep accounts and the service secure, prevent abuse and fix faults — our legitimate interests in running a safe, reliable service.
  • To improve the app using feedback you send and, if you opt in, product-use events — our legitimate interests (feedback) and your consent (optional usage information).
  • To keep compliance records — legal obligations and legitimate interests.

The app's analysis is informational golf feedback. We do not use your data to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — account sign-in and our database, hosted in London (UK).
  • Google Cloud — hosting of the app's server, in London (UK).
  • GolfCourseAPI — when you search for a course, our server sends only the search text to look up course information. No personal data is sent.
  • Apple — distributes the app through TestFlight and the App Store. If you use TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and feedback you submit through TestFlight with us under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

These providers act on our instructions. Where a provider or its sub-processors may access data from outside the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and golf data while your account is active. When you delete your account we delete your private records and sign-in identity, except where the law requires us to keep limited records (for example, a record that a deletion request was completed, without your golf data). Courses you contributed to the shared course catalogue may remain, without your identity.

During the testing phase, test data may be reset. We will tell testers before doing so where practical.

6. Your rights

You can export a copy of your data with Export My Data and request deletion with Request account deletion in the app's Settings. You also have rights to access, correct, restrict or object to our use of your data, and to withdraw consent for optional usage information at any time. Email connor.jones@dsightcg.com to make a request.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

7. Children

The app is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access to your data is restricted to your account, and server credentials are kept out of the app. No system is perfectly secure; tell us promptly if you suspect a problem.

9. Changes

We will update this page and its date when the policy changes, and tell you in the app about significant changes.
