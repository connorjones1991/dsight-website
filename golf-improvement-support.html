Using the app

How do I review a round?

Choose Quick or Detailed review, select your course and tee, and enter a score for each hole played. Leave unplayed holes blank. Work through your shots in order, marking each great, a mistake or neither. Answer the follow-up questions about mistakes. You can save your progress and return to an unfinished review.

Completed results show improvement priorities where your recorded answers support them. If you marked great shots, add private reflections and tags to remember what went well.

What is the difference between Quick and Detailed?

Quick is free. It records shot grades and explores recurring mistake groups after the shot review. Detailed is a Premium feature that asks more about execution and decisions, such as contact, ball flight, lie and putting. Individual completed-round results and great-shot reflections remain available without Premium. Premium also unlocks the combined Analyse My Golf view.

How do reminders work?

After completing a round, choose “Remind me to reflect later” to schedule a local reminder. Settings → Notifications also has a one-off “Remind me to review my round” option and reminder controls. Allow notifications in iPhone Settings if requested. Reminders are optional and free; delivery depends on iOS notification settings.

How do I set a leave-the-club reminder?

Open Settings → Notifications → When I leave my golf club. Search for and select your club, then turn the reminder on. Allow notifications and location “While Using the App”. Apple Maps receives the search words; the selected club's name, address and coordinates stay in secure storage on your phone.

iOS monitors a region normally 3 km around the club, capped by the device's supported radius, and delivers a reminder when you leave it. The app does not read or upload your current location. If you stay within that area, for example because you live nearby, a reminder may not arrive. iOS controls timing; this is not a precise departure alarm.

Switch it off in Notifications to cancel it. The selected club remains saved locally. Account deletion currently does not clear this setting or cancel all local reminders, so switch it off and cancel pending reminders before deleting your account. You can also turn off the app's notification permission in iPhone Settings.

Can I change measurements?

Open Settings → Measurements. Choose yards or metres for course lengths, and feet or centimetres for putting. Longer metric putts use metres where the entry screen says so. These are device preferences; follow the units shown beside each field.

How do analysis filters work?

Use the round and period controls on the analysis, putting and mistake-map screens. Periods include Last month, 3 months, Last year, 5 years, All time and a custom start date. The start date is included and uses when you played the round. Round counts and available evidence still limit what is shown: All time does not remove the screen's loading limits. Return to All time or widen the round selection if expected results are missing.

Scoring trends need complete 18-hole scorecards. Putting results need recorded putting evidence; a filter changes the view, not your saved rounds.

Can I change my date of birth or other account details?

You can change your display name, handedness and handicap choices in Settings. Your date of birth and sign-in email are shown read-only. To correct either, email support or use Settings → Privacy and your data → Request a correction.

How do I turn off usage sharing?

Open Settings → Help improve the app and turn sharing off. This stops new optional usage events and deletes stored events and the legacy presentation assignment. It does not delete your golf records, voluntary priority feedback or the record of your choice.

Subscriptions and billing

What does Premium include, and how does the trial work?

Premium unlocks new Detailed reviews and Analyse My Golf. Monthly and annual auto-renewable plans are available. Eligible new subscribers can start the annual plan with a 14-day free trial; the monthly plan has no free trial. Check Membership for Apple's current eligibility, price and billing period. After the trial, the selected plan renews automatically at the price shown before purchase.

Apple handles payment. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Renewal is charged within 24 hours before the end of the current period. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you purchase a subscription, where applicable.

How do I cancel?

In the app, open Settings → Membership → Manage subscriptions, or open iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → The Golf Improvement App. Follow Apple's cancellation steps. Removing the app, signing out or deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription.

How do I restore purchases?

Sign in to the same app account and use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Settings → Membership → Restore purchases. Allow an internet connection for verification. If access does not return, check that the subscription is active with Apple and contact support; do not send your Apple password.

Can I use an offer code?

If you have an offer code, open Settings → Membership and choose Redeem an offer code, or follow the redemption link that came with the code. Check Apple's eligibility, expiry and renewal terms before accepting. If access does not update, use Restore purchases in Membership.

Delete your account

First cancel any Apple subscription separately and export data you want to keep from Settings → Privacy and your data → Export My Data. Switch off the club reminder and cancel pending reminders in Notifications.

Choose Settings → Delete account and confirm. Successful deletion immediately removes your sign-in identity and linked private app records, including rounds, reviews, reflections, lessons and membership records. It is permanent. Shared course information remains without your retained submitter attribution; other golfers' data is unaffected. The Privacy Policy explains operational records, backups and device settings that are not instantly erased.

If an error appears, do not assume deletion succeeded. Retry or contact support. If the app reports that some device data could not be cleared, restart it as instructed.
