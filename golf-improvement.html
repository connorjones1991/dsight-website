iPhone app

The Golf Improvement App

Play your round, review it after. Find the most important fixes that cut your score fastest and use Reflections to build confidence.

There is nothing to enter during your round, no phone to check between shots and no sensor to wear. Afterwards, you walk back through your shots and the app turns what you tell it into the insight a coach would ask for.

How it works

Review after the round, not during it

Review each shot in order and mark it as great, a mistake or neither. Detailed review goes further, asking about contact, ball flight, lie, decisions and putting: the questions a coach would ask.

See which misses cost you the most strokes, how often they happen and the patterns that come with them, by club, area of the game, lie and strike. These are patterns in what you recorded, not a diagnosis, but they give you something specific to check in practice.

See which parts of your game to work on first, backed by the shots you recorded. Each priority comes with a practice tip you can use at your next session.

Use Reflections to give your best shots your full attention, and revisit them before your next tee time to start with more confidence.

Set an optional reminder to review your round that evening, or when you leave your golf club. Reminders can be turned off in Settings.

Free to start

Your first three rounds are free

As a first-time user, you get every review mode for your first three rounds, with no subscription or payment details needed.

  • Quick review
  • Detailed review
  • Individual round results
  • Great-shot Reflections
  • Review reminders

An account is required. For golfers aged 13 and over.
