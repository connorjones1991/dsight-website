Current project

Golf Improvement App

DSIGHT is currently developing a mobile application designed to help golfers perform structured reviews of their rounds and identify the issues having the greatest impact on performance.

The product focuses on post-round reflection rather than requiring golfers to record extensive statistics during play.

Users review relevant shots and decisions from a round through structured question flows. The application then organises recurring issues into meaningful improvement priorities.

The system is designed to distinguish between areas such as technical execution, strategy, decision-making and mental performance.

Over multiple rounds, the product is intended to help golfers recognise recurring patterns and direct practice towards the issues that appear most consistently.

The application is currently under active development and is not yet commercially available.
